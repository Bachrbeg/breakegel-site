How long do Kegel exercises take to work?

Updated November 10, 2026 · Reading time: 5 min

In short: with 3 daily sessions, the first results of Kegel exercises usually appear after 4 to 6 weeks (fewer stress leaks, better sensation) and become clear after 3 months. Progress depends mainly on consistency and on the quality of the squeeze, not on strength.

The realistic timeline

PeriodWhat you may notice
Weeks 1–2You locate the muscles more easily; the squeeze becomes cleaner. No visible effect yet.
Weeks 3–4The hold goes from 3 to 5 seconds without shaking. First improvement in urgency.
Weeks 4–6Fewer stress leaks (coughing, laughing, sport). A stronger feeling of "support".
Months 2–3Clear results for continence. Effects on sexual function (erection, orgasm, control).
After 3 monthsMaintenance phase: 1 to 2 sessions a day are enough to keep the gains.

These timings are the ones seen in pelvic-floor rehabilitation programmes; they vary from person to person depending on the starting point, age and the cause of the problem.

What speeds progress up

  • Consistency: three short sessions every day rather than one long one now and then. This is factor number one.
  • Quality of the squeeze: lift inward, no bearing down, no clenching of buttocks or belly, keep breathing.
  • Complete relaxation between squeezes: a muscle that never relaxes does not get stronger.
  • Progression: lengthen the hold (3 → 5 → 7 → 10 seconds) and move from lying to sitting to standing.
  • Tracking: ticking off successful days clearly improves adherence.

What slows it down

  • Skipping days: by far the most common reason for no result.
  • Squeezing the wrong muscles (glutes, abs). A professional can check this in one session.
  • Overdoing it: an overactive pelvic floor becomes painful and can worsen some problems.
  • A cause that Kegels alone can't fix: significant prolapse, infection, neurological condition. See a doctor if nothing changes after 3 months.

How to tell you are progressing

Three simple markers: how long you can hold without shaking (3 → 10 seconds), the number of leak-free days, and how easily you can squeeze while standing. Frequently asked questions

How many weeks before Kegels show an effect?

Usually 4 to 6 weeks for urine leaks, with 3 daily sessions. Effects on sexual function take about 3 months.

What if I see no result after 3 months?

First have your technique checked by a physiotherapist or midwife. If the squeeze is correct and regular, see a doctor to look for another cause.

Do I have to keep doing Kegels for life?

Ideally yes, as maintenance: 1 to 2 short sessions a day are enough once you have results.

Do the results disappear if I stop?

As with any muscle, tone gradually decreases after a few weeks without exercise. Restarting brings you back to your level faster than the first time.

Important: this guide is informational and does not replace medical advice. If you have pain, significant leaks or any doubt, see a doctor, midwife or physiotherapist.
